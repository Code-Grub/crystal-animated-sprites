# Changelog

## 0.4.3

- Fixes thin lines that could run through the Pokemon on the title screen on some displays.

## 0.4.2

- The Pokemon on the title screen is drawn in full colour even where it reaches toward Red, instead of turning purple there.

## 0.4.0

- A `SPRITE COLORS` option. `CRYSTAL` (the default) draws each Pokemon in the colours Crystal gave it, and `GAME` keeps the colours of the game you are playing. Crystal colours only show when the game's COLORS setting is ADVANCED.
- Crystal's small party icons in the party menu and the PC boxes, with a `PARTY ICONS` option (`CRYSTAL` by default, `GAME` for the original icons). They work with Bill's PC Plus and are drawn in the game's colours.
- The sprites rebuild once on the first launch after updating, and take about 30 MB.

## 0.3.0

- The status screen plays the animation, like the Pokedex entry.
- The Pokedex entry animates with the Battle Art Voxel mod too.

## 0.2.2

- The DIAGNOSTICS readout also shows whether the Pokedex entry animation is running and which screens were opened last.

## 0.2.1

- The Pokedex entry animation also works when another mod replaces the entry screen.

## 0.2.0

- Crystal's picture replaces the front sprite on the status screen, the Pokedex, the evolution screen, the Hall of Fame, the title screen, Prof. Oak's intro, trades and the credits. The Pokedex entry plays the animation and the others show the resting frame.
- Back pictures on those screens and trainer pictures are unchanged.

## 0.1.4

- Gaps in the sprites, such as between Pidgey's feet, will stay see-through in the 3D battle mods (Potato Voxel and Battle Art Voxel) once they adopt the matching change. Until then they behave as before.

## 0.1.3

- A `DIAGNOSTICS` option, off by default, shows a few lines about the mod over the battle screen. It is for bug reports on devices where the save folder cannot be opened.

## 0.1.2

- White patches that were really gaps in the sprites, such as between limbs or inside a hood, are now see-through, including in animation poses and on back sprites. 74 front and 91 back sprites were also touched up by hand.
- The sprites rebuild once from your ROM on the first launch.

## 0.1.1

- Sprites no longer have a white rectangle around them, so they sit cleanly on mods that replace the battle background. The sprites rebuild once on the first launch.
- A stray white pixel at the base of Pikachu's tail is cleared.

## 0.1.0

- Enemy Pokemon play Crystal's own battle animation in Red, Blue and Yellow, for all 151 Kanto species.
- A `BACK SPRITES` option: `CRYSTAL` (the default) shows Crystal's back sprite and `ANIMATED FRONT` shows the animated front sprite, mirrored.
- The sprites are built from your own Pokemon Crystal ROM the first time the mod runs. No artwork is included.
